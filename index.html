Frequently Asked Questions

A few honest answers before you call or stop by.

Do you work on my specific European make?

We specialize in BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Land Rover, Porsche, Audi and Volvo, and we also service other foreign and domestic vehicles. Call ahead with your make and model and we can tell you right away whether it's a fit.

Can you help with a California smog check?

Yes. We handle smog tests as well as the repair work needed to get a vehicle to pass, so it can typically be taken care of in one visit.

What kind of routine maintenance do you handle?

Tune-ups, oil changes, air conditioning service, exhaust and electrical repair, and drivetrain work are all part of our day-to-day. If you're not sure whether something falls under routine maintenance, just give us a call.

Do I need an appointment?

Calling ahead at (619) 282-5555 helps us plan the bay and parts for your visit, especially for specialty European work. It's the fastest way to get an honest estimate of drop-off timing.

Do you sell used cars too?

Yes, alongside our repair work we also offer used car sales. Ask us what's currently available when you call or visit the shop.
